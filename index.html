Using the app

Keep the essentials close.

Your plan and your reservation

A visit plan, favourite or calendar entry helps you organise your day. It does not reserve a time slot or confirm payment. Use the booking link and keep the confirmation from the location’s booking process.

Saved information

Favourites, plans and checklist progress are kept on your device. They are not automatically shared with the booking team or synced between devices. Details you choose to export are handled by the app or calendar service you select.

Staff and Contratos

Authorised Marbella staff can open the existing Contratos portal and use its normal authentication. The separate training workspace contains fictional examples. Training entries do not create real sales, payments or contracts. Sotogrande live staff access is not included.

Getting help

Include your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. Keep identity documents, signatures, payment details and customer contract records out of screenshots sent for routine app support.

Your information, explained.

See how local plans, calendar exports and website links work in our app privacy notice. For questions about an existing booking or contract, contact the relevant location team.
