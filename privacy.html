What this notice covers

This notice describes the Marbella JetSki mobile app published by Genesis Dynamics SL. The app lets you browse experiences, keep favourites, prepare visit plans and open the existing booking or staff services.

Existing bookings, payments and signed contracts continue to be handled by the relevant business and its service providers. Their privacy information applies to those records. This notice does not replace the activity operator’s booking terms or contract notices.

Information saved on your device

The app stores your selected location, favourite activity choices, preparation checklist progress and visit plans locally. A plan may contain an activity, date, time, guest count and a booking reference that you choose to enter. Local history and timestamps let the app retain those choices.

Saving a plan does not send it to the booking team. The app does not provide its own cloud account or automatically sync these preferences between devices. The device’s backup and restore settings may affect local app information.

Changing a favourite or checklist changes what you see. Earlier local history may remain. The current app does not include an in-app erasure control, and it does not automatically remove existing booking or contract records.

When you choose to share or contact

Calendar export creates a tentative visit file containing the activity, time, meeting location, guest count, explanatory notes and any booking reference you saved in the plan. A file is placed in the app’s local cache and the device’s sharing interface lets you choose a destination. The receiving calendar or other app handles the copy you send to it.

Phone, email, WhatsApp and maps buttons open the relevant service when you select them. Those services handle communications, website requests and any information you decide to provide under their own policies. A meeting-point link contains the business destination; the app does not request your device location for that link.

Booking and the staff portal

Booking links open the relevant location’s existing website through the system browser interface. Information entered there is processed by that website and its providers. Website cookies, logs, payments and confirmation messages belong to that existing workflow.

The Marbella staff link opens the existing Contratos portal. Authorised staff use its existing authentication. The app does not bypass that authentication, automatically import its records into local plans or copy them into the training workspace. The portal’s own records, session and handling of documents remain with that service.

Consult the location’s privacy notice and contact the team about existing records:

  • Marbella website privacy information
  • Sotogrande website privacy information

A separate staff training workspace

Training examples use fictional names, bookings, prices and contract states. A training draft stays in the local training workspace and is not a reservation, payment or legally signed contract.

Use fictional details when practising. The workspace is not intended for participant identity documents, real signatures or sensitive customer information. Live staff access for Sotogrande is not part of this version.

Permissions and app telemetry

The app does not ask for camera, microphone, photo-library, contacts or GPS access for its local planning features. Calendar export uses a file-sharing action rather than reading your existing calendar.

No advertising, behavioural analytics or custom automatic crash-upload service is configured in the app. It makes no automatic booking or contract requests. Websites you choose to open, services you choose to contact and device platform services may process information according to their own settings and notices.

Questions about your information

For an app privacy question, contact the Marbella team and mention “Genesis Dynamics SL — app privacy”. For a booking or contract record, contact the location responsible for that activity. The business will handle access, correction, restriction or other privacy-rights requests through its existing process. The app does not promise or perform automatic changes to those records.
